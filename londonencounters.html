Double-Decker Bus
AC HD No. Attacks Damage Move No. Appearing Save As Treasure Type Morale XP
15 20d10 N/A N/A Max 500' 1 N/A None N/A 10
A gigantic two story metal ship that moves around on the land. Tourists enjoy riding them around the city to stare at various large buildings. They will be carrying 2d10 tourists at any one time (treat as tourists and a driver (treat as a cabbie. There will be a pickpocket on the bus if there are more than ten riders.

Hackney Cab
AC HD No. Attacks Damage Move No. Appearing Save As Treasure Type Morale XP
8 1d6 +2 Whip 1d6 25' (60' on horseback) 1 Fighter 2 L2 10 25
Hackney cabs give people romantic rides in horse-drawn carts. Their hackney license does not allow them to stray far from the park.

Police Officers
AC HD No. Attacks Damage Move No. Appearing Save As Treasure Type Morale XP
9 3d6 Billy Club, .38 revolver 1d4 subdual; 1d8/1d6 25' 1d4 Fighter 2 L1 5 (10 if in a group of 6 or more) 75
The city guards of London, often referred to as "bobbies". Police are equipped with firearms and devices which allow them to summon an additional 1d4 comrades within five minutes during the day (5% chance of arrival within 3 turns) and 10 minutes at night. They tend to look down on "tourists" and may confront individuals who look or behave strangely.


Punk Rockers
AC HD No. Attacks Damage Move No. Appearing Save As Treasure Type Morale XP
10 3d6 +1 switchblade (as dagger), Brass knuckles 1d4 slashing, 1d4+2 bludgeoning 25' 1d6 Fighter 2 L2 10 35
A community of people who listen to an unpleasant thudding style of music. Members are friendly despite being dirty and resembling grown-up street urchins.

Reporters
AC HD No. Attacks Damage Move No. Appearing Save As Treasure Type Morale XP
8 2d4 Unarmed 0-2 40' 1d2, rises by 1d2 for every subsequent encounter Thief 3 L2 12 50
Reporters are a kind of town crier who watch happenings in London and then speak into a device which disseminates their voice across the city. Reporters may or may not inform the police directly of a subject's whereabouts, but the bobbies listen to their broadcasts, too...

5 1d4 1 slingshot (as sling) 0-1 50' 1d10 Thief 1 L3 3 10
A child beggar. Urchins move in large groups which commit petty crimes. They are very skittish but friendly to anti-authority figures such as people who fight police.

Tourists

5 1d6 Unarmed 0-2 25' 5d10 daytime, 1d4 nighttime Thief 1 L2 5 30
Travelers from distant lands. There is only a 75% chance that any particular tourist speaks English.

Truant Officer
AC HD No. Attacks Damage Move No. Appearing Save As Treasure Type Morale XP
8 1d8 1 billy club 1d4 subdual 30' 2 Fighter 2 L1 7 50
Truant officers are special police officers tasked with rounding up street urchins and forcing them to go to a torture dungeon (school). They are generally unsympathetic to pleas that the weather is too nice to be inside, that it's Sunday, and so on.
