Special Events

These are events which are somewhat unusual, even on the streets of London. Characters who have traveled from other times and places may find them more alarming or disorienting than anything else they are likely to ever see during their sojourn in this city.

Traffic Accident
A three-car pileup started by a careless Lamborghini driver rear-ending a bus. 50/50 chance that the drivers are being loaded into ambulances or getting in a fistfight (treat as locals).

Fire
A large fire has broken out which has immolated one building and it spreading to an adjacent one. There are 3d10 locals, 1d10 tourists, 1d2 reporters, ten bobbies holding back the crowd, and 1d3 firetrucks with five firefighters (treat as bobbies) who are trying to put out the fire.

Fire Truck
Two firetrucks go screaming by to a fire elsewhere. Anyone within earshot will hear the sirens two turns (20 seconds) before they come into view.

Gang Battle
Two evenly matched gangs of 4d5 gang members are brawling. There is a 50% chance that a gang member will flee if he endures more than 3hp of damage. Half of them have their girlfriends (as hookers) and 1d4 winos watching and cheering them on. Reroll if it is daytime.

Plane
A traffic helicopter or low-flying jet passes by overhead.

Mugging
Two tourists are being robbed by a mugger.

Terrorist Encounter
The PCs encounter terrorists. Roll 1d3: 1: Irish, 2: Libyans, 3: Communists. Their reaction to the PCs is contingent on the reputation they have accrued in the city, with a 50% chance that their movements have been noted if they have had any kind of adverse interaction with the police.
Burglary
Three burglars (2x gangsters and 1 mugger) are breaking into a house or business. During the day the heroes spot them running out of a local business with armfuls of merchandise.
Loose Dog
A dogwalker with 2d3 pit bulls loses control of the animals. There is a 75% chance that they will turn on the walker and 25% chance that they will go after the PCs.
